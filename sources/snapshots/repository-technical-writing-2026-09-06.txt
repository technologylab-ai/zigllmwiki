Repository prose follows **Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100), guided by Zinsser**. One idea per sentence, at most 20 words (25 for descriptions). Active voice, and name who acts. One word, one meaning — never the same word as a noun and a verb. Define every technical term at its first use, and pick one term per concept and keep it. Every "that", "this", and "it" needs a clear noun. The standard never governs code identifiers, quoted output, error strings, or exact-format text.
